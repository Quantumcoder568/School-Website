About Royal Wisdom High School

Excellence in education since 1985

Founded in 1985, Royal Wisdom High School has been at the forefront of academic excellence, character development, and holistic education. Our institution has nurtured generations of leaders, innovators, and compassionate citizens who make meaningful contributions to society.

We believe that every student possesses unique talents and potential. Our mission is to provide an environment where students can discover their passions, develop critical thinking skills, and build the character needed to thrive in an ever-changing world.

Our Mission

To empower students with knowledge, character, and critical thinking skills through a rigorous academic program complemented by arts, athletics, and community service, preparing them for success in college and purposeful lives.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution that inspires lifelong learning, fosters global citizenship, and develops innovative leaders who will positively impact their communities and the world.

Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, Respect, Innovation, and Community. These core values guide everything we do and form the foundation of our school culture.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the student-teacher ratio?

Our student-teacher ratio is 12:1, ensuring personalized attention and support for every student.

Do you offer financial aid?

Yes, we offer need-based financial aid and merit scholarships. Approximately 30% of our students receive some form of financial assistance.

What are the school hours?

School hours are from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, with after-school programs available until 6:00 PM.

Is transportation provided?

Yes, we offer bus services covering most areas in the city. Transportation fees apply.
